# the channel explainer look

Studied from Trust Me Bro, *Would You Survive a Nuclear Blast?* (20.6 min, 1280×720, 60fps, **213 hard cuts**). Adjacent, not a clone. No red cap. No Brody.

## What the reference actually does

- A 2D sticker host over simple sets, black voids, and occasional photo/3D plates.
- Title card: black field, huge condensed grotesque, one word in fire-engine red, host peeking at the bottom.
- Diagrams: a row of flat icons on black plus one red scale mark.
- Extra people: same body language, different shirt color.
- Face is 2 black dots + a mouth, or a blank white circle. Never a realistic face.
- Average shot ~6 seconds. Plenty of 2-second punches. Title can hold longer.
